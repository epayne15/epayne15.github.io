Plan Your Visit

A little preparation can make live events more enjoyable. Use these steps to plan your trip.

Event-day checklist

  1. Confirm event time, ticket details and bag policy.
  2. Plan transportation and allow extra time for traffic.
  3. Check seating, accessibility and weather.
  4. Set a budget for food, parking and merchandise.

Motorsports

Bring hearing protection, check the weather and plan your walk from parking to the grandstand.

Festivals

Review the event map, locate water stations and choose a meeting place with your group.
